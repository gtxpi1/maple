# 01 — Vision & Mission (Drafted v0.1)

> Original library status: "Planned." No dedicated source document existed;
> this is extracted and expanded from the Master Blueprint's vision statement.
> Treat as a first draft, not final.

## Vision

Build the world's most configurable marketplace platform: a single codebase
capable of running one marketplace or thousands, each with independent
branding, categories, themes, pricing, languages, moderation rules, payment
methods, and features.

## Core Principle

**"Familiar to use. Original by design."**

The product should feel immediately usable to anyone who has used a classifieds
or marketplace site before, while being built from original code, branding,
and assets — not a clone or fork of an existing platform.

## Mission (draft — needs stakeholder confirmation)

Give operators — from a single hobbyist site to a network of regional
marketplaces — a platform they fully control: self-hosted, configurable from
the admin panel without code changes, and secure by default.

## Open Questions

- Target operator profile: solo/small business vs. enterprise/multi-brand network?
- Target end-user: general classifieds, or a specific vertical (e.g. local goods, services)?
- Success metrics for v1.0 (adoption, listings/day, marketplaces deployed)?
